A quick visual reference to the pinout (wiring) of the [Electronic Control Unit](/vw-type-2-ecu-part-number-reference) (ECU) used in the 1975-1979 VW Type 2 bus, from the harness side. The ECU was at the heart of the fuel injection system Volkswagen chose to reduce emissions and for better fuel economy: Bosch's [L-Jetronic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jetronic#L-Jetronic_(1974%E2%80%931989)).
