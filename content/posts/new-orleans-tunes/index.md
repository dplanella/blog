Ten tracks from modern New Orleans bands that bring the old sound and joy for the music to the present, including Cassidy & The Orleans Kids, Smoking Time Jazz Club, Tuba Skinny and more.

This mix was brought to you with lots of love (and Open Source). Enjoy!

\

### Track list

1.  Moan You Moaners *by The Palmetto Bug Stompers*
2.  The End of the Day (An Absence of Gin) *by Laique*
3.  Mr. Jelly Lord *by The Smoking Time Jazz Club*
4.  Joseph Joseph *by Meschiya Lake and the Little Big Horns*
5.  Salty Dog *by Cassidy & The Orleans Kids*
6.  You Rascal, You *by Baby Soda*
7.  You Gonna Quit Me *by Big Nasty*
8.  Blues My Naughty Sweetie Gives To Me *by Ingrid Lucia & the Flying Neutrinos*
9.  Crow Jane *by Tuba Skinny*
10. Mama's Gone Goodbye *by Shotgun Jazz Band*

Also on [8tracks](http://8tracks.com/david-planella/new-orleans-tunes#smart_id=dj:2565862).
