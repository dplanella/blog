Just a quick reminder that the next [Ubuntu App Developer Week](https://davidplanella.wordpress.com/2011/03/31/announcing-ubuntu-app-developer-week/) starts **next Monday** on the [\#ubuntu-classroom](http://webchat.freenode.net/?channels=ubuntu-classroom) IRC channel on Freenode.

So get ready for a week packed with sessions on how to develop awesome applications in Ubuntu, straight from the best experts!

Here's a sneak peek at the sessions the week is kicking off with:

- [16:00 UTC](http://www.timeanddate.com/worldclock/fixedtime.html?month=4&day=11&year=2011&hour=16&min=0&sec=0&p1=0) - **Enabling Multitouch and Gestures Using uTouch** with [Chase Douglas](https://wiki.ubuntu.com/ChaseDouglas) and [Stephen Webb](https://wiki.ubuntu.com/bregma)
- [17:00 UTC](http://www.timeanddate.com/worldclock/fixedtime.html?month=4&day=11&year=2011&hour=17&min=0&sec=0&p1=0) - **GObject Introspection: The New Way For Developing GNOME Apps in Python, JavaScript and Others** with [Tomeu Vizoso](http://blog.tomeuvizoso.net/ "LaunchpadHome")
- [18:00 UTC](http://www.timeanddate.com/worldclock/fixedtime.html?month=4&day=11&year=2011&hour=18&min=0&sec=0&p1=0) - **From English to Any Language: Internationalizing Your Apps** with [David Planella](https://launchpad.net/%7Edpm "LaunchpadHome")
- [19:00 UTC](http://www.timeanddate.com/worldclock/fixedtime.html?month=4&day=11&year=2011&hour=19&min=0&sec=0&p1=0) - **Widgetcraft: The Art of Creating Plasma Widgets** with [Harald Sitter](https://launchpad.net/%7Eapachelogger "LaunchpadHome")
- [20:00 UTC](http://www.timeanddate.com/worldclock/fixedtime.html?month=4&day=11&year=2011&hour=20&min=0&sec=0&p1=0) - **Rock solid Python development with unittest/doctest** with [Barry Warsaw](https://launchpad.net/%7Ebarry "LaunchpadHome")
- But there is [much more...](https://wiki.ubuntu.com/UbuntuAppDeveloperWeek/Timetable)

Looking forward to an awesome week. See you there!
