Just a quick note to announce that Lucid is now open for translation.

You can now go to

- <http://translations.launchpad.net/ubuntu>

and help making the Lynx speak your language.

See the [original announcement](https://lists.ubuntu.com/archives/ubuntu-translators/2010-February/003240.html) for more details.

*If you need any help in joining a team, have any questions regarding Ubuntu Translations, or simply want to get in touch with other members of the global Ubuntu Translations community you can [contact the Translations team](https://wiki.ubuntu.com/Translations/Contact/). You can  also [report translations bugs](https://wiki.ubuntu.com/Bugs/HowToTriage#Translation%20Bugs%20and%20Launchpad%20integration) in the [Ubuntu Translations project](https://bugs.launchpad.net/ubuntu-translations) in Launchpad\*
