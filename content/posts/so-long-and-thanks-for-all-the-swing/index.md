A mix to bid farewell to Valencia, to say "hi!" to Stuttgart, and to thank some awesome people for the many, many hours of swing danced and played there.

<iframe height="120" src="https://www.mixcloud.com/widget/iframe/?hide_cover=1&amp;light=1&amp;feed=%2Fdplanella%2Fso-long-and-thanks-for-all-the-swing%2F" width="720"></iframe>

\

I've picked a selection of songs from the latest DJ sessions: a mixture of new and old swing, with the usual New Orleans tunes and a tint of gipsy. But most of all, music to bring good vibes.

Enjoy!

Also on [8tracks](http://8tracks.com/david-planella/so-long-and-thanks-for-all-the-swing).

8.  He's Smilin' Too *by The Cope St Parade*
