IRC rockstar [Jussi Schultink](http://jussi01.com/ "Jussi01.com » Ubuntu"), on behalf of the [Ubuntu IRC Council](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-irc-council) tells me that the [Ubuntu IRC Terms of Service](https://wiki.ubuntu.com/IRC/TermsOfService) are up for translation, so feel free to go to <https://wiki.ubuntu.com/IRC/TermsOfService> and start a new translation in your language.

You'll find instructions on how to do it at the bottom of the page, so there's no excuse to make them available in everyone's language!
