I wanted to let you know of a project which probably won't be new to the most of you.

[Ubuntu Netbook Remix](https://wiki.ubuntu.com/UNR) (in short, UNR) is a sleek, easy to use and yet powerful version of Ubuntu aimed at [netbook](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netbook) devices and... well of course, human beings.

What you might not have known is that as with Ubuntu, you can translate and use UNR in your very own language. The [UNR project](https://translations.launchpad.net/unr) has already been translated to some [36 languages](https://translations.launchpad.net/netbook-remix-launcher), but there are still some in need of love.

So, if you are a speaker of [Belarusian](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-l10n-by), [Bengali](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-l10n-bn), [Bosnian](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-l10n-bs), [Bulgarian](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-l10n-bg), [Dhivehi](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-l10n-dv), [Estonian](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-l10n-et), [Filipino](https://launchpad.net/~loco-philippine-team), [Hindi](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-l10n-hi), [Irish](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-l10n-ie), [Lithuanian](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-l10n-lt), [Nepali](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-l10n-ne), [Serbian](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-l10n-sr), [Vietnamese](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-l10n-vi), [Welsh](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-l10n-cy), [Xhosa](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-l10n-xh), [Zulu](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-zulu) or [any of these other languages](https://translations.launchpad.net/+groups/ubuntu-translators) without a translation already, here's what you can do to make UNR speak your tongue:

- Contact [your localisation team](https://translations.launchpad.net/+groups/ubuntu-translators) and let them know you'd like to translate UNR. They will help you getting up to speed on how they work and on how to use [Launchpad Translations](https://launchpad.net/+tour/translation).

And who knows, maybe when you've finished you might feel like translating some more of these other [awesome projects](https://translations.launchpad.net/)!
