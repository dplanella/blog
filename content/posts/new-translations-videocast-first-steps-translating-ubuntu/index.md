<img src="/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/ul10n-tv.png" title="Ubuntu Translations TV" class="alignright size-full wp-image-702" width="172" height="153" alt="Ubuntu Translations TV" />After the [first Ubuntu Translations videocast](http://www.ustream.tv/recorded/11209840) two weeks ago, [join me tomorrow](http://www.ustream.tv/channel/ubuntu-translations-with-david-planella) in a new edition where I'll talk about the first steps to get you started translating Ubuntu.

So if are either:

- new to Ubuntu and would like to learn more and join the awesome translations community...
- not involved in translations but would like to know more about how Ubuntu is translated...
- an experienced translator that would like to ask questions or share your workflow...

... then this show is definitely for you.

Come along tomorrow and watch the introduction on how to get started, the general translations workflow, and participate by asking your questions!

- **WHAT**: Videocast - *First Steps Translating Ubuntu*
- **WHEN**: [Thursday, Dec. 16th, 16:00 UTC](http://timeanddate.com/worldclock/fixedtime.html?year=2010&month=12&day=16&hour=16&min=0&sec=0)
- **WHERE**: [Ustream Ubuntu Translations channel](http://www.ustream.tv/channel/ubuntu-translations-with-david-planella)

*Note that if you wish to participate in the online chat, you’ll need to [sign up](http://www.ustream.tv/login-signup?ref=%2Fdashboard) for a ustream account (it doesn’t take more than a couple of minutes), but I’ll also be answering your questions on the [\#ubuntu-translators](http://webchat.freenode.net/?channels=ubuntu-translators) IRC channel on Freenode.*

Talk to you all tomorrow!
