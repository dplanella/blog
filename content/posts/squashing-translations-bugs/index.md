## How can you help with translations bugs?

Remember that you don't have to wait for a Hug Day to help with Ubuntu Translations bugs, you can contribute any day!

Here are a couple of things you can give a hand with:

- [Reporting translations bugs](https://help.ubuntu.com/community/ReportingBugs#Filing%20translation%20bugs)
- Triaging the [existing bugs](https://bugs.launchpad.net/ubuntu-translations) at the [Ubuntu Translations project](https://launchpad.net/ubuntu-translations) in Launchpad
- [Subscribing to Ubuntu Translations bugmail](https://launchpad.net/ubuntu-translations/+subscribe)
