[<img src="/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/ul10n-tv.png?w=300" title="Ubuntu Translations TV" class="alignright size-medium wp-image-702" width="180" height="160" alt="Ubuntu Translations TV" />](/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/ul10n-tv.png)Ladies and gents, I'm pleased to announce the next [Ubuntu Translations videocast](http://www.ustream.tv/channel/ubuntu-translations-with-david-planella) **tomorrow** from Dallas, Texas, where this week we are holding the Canonical Platform Rally for the next version of Ubuntu, the Natty Narwhal.

This time around I'll have the privilege to be joined by ﻿﻿[Danilo Šegan](https://launchpad.net/~danilo) (or his alter ego Данило Шеган), the [Launchpad Translations](https://translations.launchpad.net/) developer team lead.

Those of you involved in translations will know Danilo well, not only for his work in developing the translations application in Launchpad, but also for his community involvement. A regular at [UDS](http://uds.ubuntu.com/) and [GUADEC](http://guadec.org) conferences, he's also developed and maintained some of the key tools in the Free Software Localization ecosystem, such as [xml2po](http://live.gnome.org/GnomeDocUtils) and [intltool](https://launchpad.net/intltool).

He'll be explaining all the cool new things coming up in Launchpad Translations, such as better [upstream integration](http://danilo.segan.org/blog/launchpad/automatic-translations-sharing), and will also tell us a bit more about other [changes affecting the way Launchpad is being developed](http://blog.launchpad.net/general/changing-how-we-track-launchpads-bugs-questions-and-blueprints).

As usual, we'll be taking and answering your questions, so come and join us for a chat!

- **WHAT**: Videocast - *Launchpad Translations News*
- **WHEN**: [Thursday, Jan. 13th, 21:00 UTC](http://timeanddate.com/worldclock/fixedtime.html?year=2011&month=1&day=13&hour=21&min=0&sec=0)
- **WHERE**: [Ustream Ubuntu Translations channel](http://www.ustream.tv/channel/ubuntu-translations-with-david-planella)

*Note that if you wish to participate in the online chat, you’ll need to [sign up](http://www.ustream.tv/login-signup?ref=%2Fdashboard) for a ustream account (it doesn’t take more than a couple of minutes), but we’ll also be answering your questions on the [\#ubuntu-translators](http://webchat.freenode.net/?channels=ubuntu-translators) IRC channel on Freenode.*

Talk to you all tomorrow!
