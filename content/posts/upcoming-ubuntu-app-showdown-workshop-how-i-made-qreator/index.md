[<img src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/qreator-128.png" title="Qreator" class="alignleft size-full wp-image-1539" width="128" height="128" />](http://launchpad.net/qreator)Just a quick reminder that I'll be running a [Workshop](http://developer.ubuntu.com/showdown/workshops/) for the [Ubuntu App Showdown](http://developer.ubuntu.com/showdown) ~~in very few minutes~~. **Update**: the video is now live!

I'll be talking about [Qreator](http://www.omgubuntu.co.uk/2012/06/qreator-offers-fast-creation-of-qr-codes-in-ubuntu), an application I developed for quick creation of [QR codes](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QR_code) from the desktop. The idea is to explain how I put it together in order to help new app developers with an example where they can learn from and ask questions about.

So feel free to [join me on the live feed, learn more and ask your questions](http://developer.ubuntu.com/showdown/workshops/classroom).

[<img src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/qreator-workshop1.png" title="How I Made Qreator" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-1536" width="230" height="230" />](http://developer.ubuntu.com/showdown/workshops)

If you cannot follow it live, [you can also see the recording](http://developer.ubuntu.com/showdown/workshops) at your own time later on.

See you there in a bit!
