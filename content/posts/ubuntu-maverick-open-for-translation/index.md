I'm pleased to announce that Ubuntu Maverick is now open for translation:

[<img src="/wp-content/uploads/2010/07/translate-button.png" title="Translate Ubuntu" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-469" width="313" height="57" alt="https://translations.launchpad.net/ubuntu/maverick" />](https://translations.launchpad.net/ubuntu/maverick)

Remember that according to the [release schedule](https://wiki.ubuntu.com/MaverickReleaseSchedule) translatable messages might be subject to change until the [User Interface Freeze](https://wiki.ubuntu.com/UserInterfaceFreeze) on the 26th of August.

During the Maverick development cycle, language packs containing the translations are generally released twice per week. This way you can see and test the results of the translations more frequently.

That's it, happy translating!
