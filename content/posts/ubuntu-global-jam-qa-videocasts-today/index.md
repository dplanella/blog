In preparation for the [Ubuntu Global Jam](http://loco.ubuntu.com/events/globaljam/) event **next weekend**, Jono and I will be [running Q+A videocasts today](http://www.jonobacon.org/2012/02/24/ubuntu-global-jam-qa-videocasts-tomorrow/)

Jono's videocast will be more convenient for the Americas and in the evening in Europe:

- [**11am Pacific / 7pm UTC/UK / 8pm CET**](http://timeanddate.com/worldclock/fixedtime.html?year=2012&month=2&day=24&hour=19&min=0&sec=0) – watch live [here](http://www.ustream.tv/channel/at-home-with-jono-bacon)!

My videocast coming up in about **2 hours time** and is more convenient for Europe and surrounding areas:

- [**12pm UTC/UK / 1pm CET / 7am Eastern**](http://timeanddate.com/worldclock/fixedtime.html?year=2012&month=2&day=24&hour=12&min=0&sec=0) – watch live [here](http://www.ustream.tv/channel/ubuntu-translations-with-david-planella)!

So if you are either thinking of organizing an event, you're already organizing one, you'd like to participate in one, or simply want to learn more, do come along, ask your questions and have some fun!

Oh, and you should also check out the video about rolling your own event: <http://youtu.be/ITk8PGBkMXQ> Can't see it? Watch it [here](http://youtu.be/ITk8PGBkMXQ)!

This Global Jam is going to be awesome, and it's going to give that extra push to make Ubuntu 12.04 even a more rock-solid release.

Have you already [signed up for an event](http://loco.ubuntu.com/events/global/1443/detail/)?

[![](/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/ugj-videocast.png "Ubuntu Global Jam Videocast")](http://www.ustream.tv/channel/ubuntu-translations-with-david-planella)

*To ask your questions on the chat, you’ll need to [sign up](http://www.ustream.tv/login-signup?ref=%2Fdashboard) for a ustream account (it's free, doesn’t take more than a couple of minutes and you can use your Launchpad OpenId), but I’ll also be answering your questions on the [\#ubuntu-locoteams](http://webchat.freenode.net/?channels=ubuntu-locoteams) IRC channel on Freenode.*
